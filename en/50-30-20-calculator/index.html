What is the 50/30/20 rule?

  1. It is a starting point for splitting your take-home pay (after taxes) into three groups.
  2. 50% for needs: what you must pay to live and work.
  3. 30% for wants: what makes life better but can be cut if needed.
  4. 20% for savings and paying off debt faster.

FAQ

What if my needs are more than 50%?

That is common, especially with high rent. Use the rule as a guide: cut wants first and adjust the percentages to your situation.

Where do debt payments go?

Minimum payments are needs. Anything extra you pay to get out of debt faster goes in the 20%.
