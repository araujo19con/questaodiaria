How the calculator works

  1. Every month, each debt grows by its interest (balance × monthly rate). If your card shows an APR, divide it by 12.
  2. You pay the minimum on every debt, and the rest of your budget goes to the target debt.
  3. Snowball: the target is the smallest balance. Avalanche: the target is the highest interest rate.
  4. When a debt is paid off, its payment rolls over to the next one.

FAQ

Which method is better?

Avalanche usually costs less interest. Snowball pays off small debts first, which helps if you need quick wins to stay motivated. The calculator shows the difference for your numbers.

My lender shows an APR. What do I enter?

Divide the APR by 12 to get an approximate monthly rate. For example, 24% APR is about 2% per month.

Are the results exact?

They are estimates. Lenders may charge daily interest and fees. Use the result to plan and check your statements.
