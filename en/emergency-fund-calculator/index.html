How big should your emergency fund be?

  • The fund is for the unexpected: losing income, medical bills, car or home repairs. Not for trips or planned purchases.
  • A common guideline is 3 to 6 months of essential expenses. With variable income (freelance, commission), many people aim higher, such as 6 to 12 months.
  • Count only essentials: housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance and minimum debt payments.
  • Keep the money somewhere low-risk that you can access quickly, such as a high-yield savings account.

FAQ

Emergency fund or debt first?

Many people build a small starter fund first, so the next surprise does not go on a credit card, and then focus on high-interest debt. The right order depends on your interest rates and how stable your income is.

Is the interest guaranteed?

No. It is only an estimate for the calculation. Real returns change with the account and over time.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a planning tool and does not guarantee any result.
