Free monthly budget template

A simple template to plan your month: income, expenses by category, planned vs. actual, what was left and how much you saved. Opens in Google Sheets, Excel and LibreOffice. No sign-up.

Download the free template (.xlsx)

What's in the free version

  • 1 month of budgeting with 3 income sources and 10 expense categories (rename them as you like)
  • Planned vs. actual, with the difference in red when you overspend
  • Money left over and savings rate calculated automatically
  • A "Paid?" column to check off your bills
Download the free template (.xlsx)

How to use it in Google Sheets

  1. Download the .xlsx file.
  2. Go to drive.google.com, click New › File upload and pick the template.
  3. Open the file and choose File › Save as Google Sheets.
  4. Type only in the yellow cells. The sample numbers are made up: delete them and enter yours.
